# Multispectral Search and Rescue Snow Goggles

## Problem
Search and resuce (SAR) workers in harsh environments (i.e. blizzards, snow storms, avalanches) have limited visibility when responding to SOS reports. 

## Proposed solution
Develop a pair of multispectral (i.e. IR, Thermal) goggles which can automatically determine which specturm gives the greatest visibility/contrast. Additionally, goggles can also have HUD which displays location to nearest search party member to assist in search efforts. 

## Why it's worth doing
Since SAR workers will already be wearing snow goggles in extreme environments, a multispectral pair of goggles could enhance successful SAR efforts by giving SAR workers enhanced visibility, especially post-avalanche or during heavy snowstorms for example. 

## Concerns and risks
- Overall package would be too bulky/heavy to be practical
- Refresh rate might not be fast enough and may cause dizzines or motion sickness
- Battery life must last long enough to be useful
- Display resolution must be high enough to allow for small/far away targets to be discernable from wildlife
