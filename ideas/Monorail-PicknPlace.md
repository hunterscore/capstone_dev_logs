# Idea name Monorail Pick and Place

## Problem
Pick and place systems for labs/manufacturing have a large footprint and cannot be contained on the workbench.
Automating work in chemical labs. Need movement across a long workbench. With modular design to replicate on multiple workbenches.

## Proposed solution
Create a monorail tripteron system that spans a long y axis.

## Decision notes
N/A

## Objective
- Pick and place parts for contained workspaces (i.e. hoods, enclosures)
- Accuracy on the xy-axis
- Affordable
- Easy to calibrate/set up
- Device should be able to fit on a workbench

## Constraints
- Device must use a linear rail
- Device must run on wall power
- Device must not deflect over 1mm
- Device must have an e-stop

## Criteria
- Volume/dimensions
- Accuracy
- Pick and place speed
- Calibration time
- Cost
