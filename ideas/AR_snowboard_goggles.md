# Idea name AR Snowboard Goggles

## Problem
Snowboarders and skiiers use apps such as Slopes and Strava to track their stats on runs and to locate their friends on other parts of the mountain. However using your phone for these apps poses several problems while on the mountain:
1. drains phone battery quickly
2. unable to check speed while on run
3. need to take off gloves and check phone to use apps
