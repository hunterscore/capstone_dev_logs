# Idea finalization and PDP preparation

## Discussion

### Needs assessment

- Completed a needs assessment for the concept.
- Identified the need for better equipment utilization in self-driving laboratories.
- Drafted the problem statement, objectives, criteria, and constraints.

### Solution concepts

- Outlined our proposal for a bench-mounted sample-handling system.
- Developed several distinct solution morphologies.

### Concept selection

- Defined the design criteria and assigned each a weight.
- Used the weights to build a decision matrix and scored each design in it.
- The monorail tripteron scored highest.

### Timeline

- Set a development timeline.

## Decisions

- The monorail tripteron is our selected design, because it scored highest in the weighted decision matrix.
